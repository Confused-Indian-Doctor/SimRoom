## Deployment

Migration `202610130004_engine_domains.sql`, the shared engine/Edge Function, authenticated Cron/Vault clock worker and Firebase Hosting deployed successfully. Website: https://cidsimroom.web.app. The worker runs every ten seconds only when sessions are running; it processes due events at their authored simulation times, with delivery latency up to a worker interval. It is not a physiological sample stream. See ENGINE-ARCHITECTURE.md for setup and ownership.

## Limitations

The four replacement faculty packs are absent. The six older STEMI/PE/PTX documents and three source cases are preserved; the opioid case, exact new twenty-item checklists, naloxone guidance, clinical reports and four-pack branches have not been invented or clinically verified. Authored fixtures test engine mechanisms, not clinical pharmacology or clinical certification. Pulse's official documentation/licence/validation scope was reviewed, but Pulse is not installed or integrated.

Physical Android/iPhone/iPad, Safari/WebKit, physical camera scanning, device audio/wake-lock and physical PWA installation were not tested. Chromium viewport tests are browser emulation. Existing Firebase pairing/drafts and autonomous clock are Supabase-only; Firebase replay uses its in-document journal and rejects replay once the full ledger is unavailable. Current checkpoints retain the most recent eight in-session snapshots; older full command records remain in the private Supabase ledger. Unsupported medicines/actions are recorded for faculty judgement without invented responses.
